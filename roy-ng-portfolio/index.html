About

Designer by craft. Consultant by mindset.

My career took me from a digital agency, through a fintech and a product startup, to consulting at Capgemini — where I led end-to-end design on a major Hong Kong retail banking application. That path taught me three very different speeds of design: agency speed, startup speed, and enterprise rigour.

What I bring isn't just screens. It's evidence-based decisions — stakeholder interviews, competitor benchmarking, guerrilla testing — translated into UI that developers can actually build. Because I code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript), my handoffs are realistic and my specs get shipped.

Lately I've been exploring how AI-assisted workflows and Web3 patterns change product design — from prompt-driven prototyping to designing for trust in decentralised products.

Retail Banking · Mobile App

Redesigning the pre-login experience of a major HK banking app

The pre-login landing page is the front door of a bank. The legacy page buried login behind cluttered promotions. I led the redesign from research to developer handoff — and the screens below show the before, the research, and the final solution.

RoleLead UX/UI (Consultant)
TeamPM · PO · Devs · Designers
Research13 competitor banks
PlatformiOS & Android

01 · Context & product screens

02 · Problem

  • Low-value content dominated the screen — promotions crowded out the actions users came for.
  • Login and registration were hard to find, generating drop-off and support calls.
  • Interaction patterns were inconsistent, built over years by different vendors.

03 · Research & evidence

  • Benchmarked 13 banks across Mainland China, Hong Kong and overseas on 4 dimensions.
  • Key insight: winners surface login + quick actions within thumb reach.

04 · Exploration → Solution

05 · Outcome

−40%*Fewer taps to reach login for returning users
8 → 1Design options iterated and converged with stakeholders
100%Screens delivered with dev-ready specs

Design Ops · Design System

From fragmented UI to a single design system

Years of vendor teams shipping without shared standards left mismatched colours, buttons and type. A big-bang redesign was impossible — so I built the escape route: a living design system.

Information architecture (foundation for the revamp)

Design system

  • Full UI audit to map inconsistencies across the product.
  • Built tokens + components, published as the team's shared design library.
  • Every new component is contributed back — consistency compounds instead of decaying.
